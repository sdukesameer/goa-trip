What changed

Corrections and open questions

Everything below was checked against published timetables, seasonal opening dates, and the actual coordinates in this file. Six things were wrong. Five more depend on the season and need a phone call before you commit to them.

Biggest risk

The entire trip sits inside the Konkan Railway monsoon timetable

The monsoon timetable runs 15 June – 20 October 2026, with speed restrictions across the Konkan route. Trains on this corridor run roughly two hours slower than the fair-weather schedule. Your 12133 leg on 26–27 September is squarely inside that window.

This also means late-September Goa is still functionally monsoon season, which drives most of the other flags on this page: the flea market, water sports, swimming, shacks, and midweek club nights.

Verify

Karmali arrival: the 08:08 needs a source

The published fair-weather timetable has 12133 reaching Karmali at 06:28. Your plan says 08:08 — which is about right for a monsoon-timetable arrival, so it is probably the number you were given, not a mistake. But it is not the number on most timetable sites, so confirm it against the Central Railway monsoon timetable or NTES before you build the morning around it.

Fair-weather TT 06:28 · your plan 08:08 · gap ≈ 1h 40m, consistent with monsoon restrictions
Fixed

Baga to Arpora is not a 30-minute ride

Plot 77 and The Hosteller Arpora are 0.7 km apart in a straight line — call it 1.5 km and five minutes by road. The original note said ~30 minutes, which would have had you leaving three hours earlier than necessary on moving day.

Day 6 transfer ~30 min → ~5 min
Fixed

Day 7 ran the northern beaches backwards

Coming from Artjuna in Anjuna, Morjim is 4.3 km out and Ashwem is 7.7 km. The original route went to Ashwem first and doubled back. Reversed, so you ride north once and turn around once.

Artjuna → Ashwem → Morjim → Morjim → Ashwem
Fixed

The Ribandar ferry does not go to Divar

Ribandar's jetty serves Chorão island. Divar is reached from the Old Goa jetty by the Viceroy's Arch, or from Naroa. Since you are already at Bom Jesus and Sé Cathedral, the Old Goa jetty is a two-minute ride away — so Day 3 now runs Fontainhas → Ribandar → Old Goa → ferry, instead of criss-crossing the river.

Ferry point Ribandar jetty → Old Goa jetty (Viceroy's Arch). Confirm the crossing is running; monsoon ferries get suspended in rough water.
Season

Anjuna Flea Market is very unlikely to be open on 30 September

30 September 2026 is indeed a Wednesday — that part of the plan was right. But the market is a dry-season operation: stalls come down for the monsoon around June and go back up in October. Sources put the reopening at "October", which puts your date on the wrong side of the line by days.

Day 5 now leads with Mapusa Market instead, which runs year-round, and keeps Anjuna as the upgrade if a phone call says the stalls are up.

Anjuna Wednesday market: confirmed by date → call ahead, plan on Mapusa
Fixed

Turtles are not nesting in September

Morjim's olive ridley nesting season runs roughly November to March. In early October you get the beach and the estuary, not the turtles. The note has been rewritten so nobody spends an afternoon looking for them.

Fixed

Departure day had no transport in it

The plan returned the scooters at 09:00 on Saturday and then, forty minutes of itinerary later, arrived at an airport 35–40 km away. Once four scooters go back you have eight people, eight bags, and no vehicle. Saturday is now its own day with a booked eight-seater in it.

Verify

Midweek club nights in early October

LPK on a Tuesday and Hammerzz on a Thursday are the two soft spots. Season proper hasn't started; monsoon and shoulder-season nightlife in North Goa is event-led and heavily weekend-weighted, and midweek can be dead or shut. Check each venue's Instagram the week before — that is where these places actually announce whether they are opening.

Checked, correct

The things that were already right

Every weekday label matches 2026: 25 Sep is a Friday, 30 Sep is a Wednesday, 3 Oct is a Saturday. The 12262 Duronto runs Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri, so a Friday departure works, and the 05:35 → 08:15 timing over 26h 40m and 1,968 km is the published schedule. Karmali is the right station to get off at, not Madgaon. GOX rather than GOI is the right assumption for the return. Four licensed riders for four scooters is exactly enough with no rotation.

2BAGA
Sun 27 Sep

Touchdown in Baga

Arrival, scooters, and one easy night. Do not schedule anything you'd be upset to lose — the train sets the clock.

~08:08
Arrive Karmali (KRMI) confirm time
Closer to Baga than Madgaon by a long way. Pre-book two SUVs or a Tempo Traveller — ~32 km, 45–60 min. Book the pickup for the actual arrival, then track the train from Ratnagiri onward and message the driver. Karmali is a small station and taxis there are a seller's market if you turn up unbooked.
~09:30
Check in, Plot 77 by HNH, Baga early check-in
Standard check-in is usually early afternoon. Get 09:30 agreed in writing, or at minimum secure luggage storage and a bathroom.
~11:00
Collect 4 scooters added detail
Video every scooter before any money changes hands — all four sides, fuel gauge, existing scratches, tyre tread, both mirrors, working horn and indicators. Count eight helmets, not four. Goa enforces pillion helmets. Photograph the RC and insurance for each bike, and do not surrender original licences or passports — offer a photocopy and a cash deposit instead.
13:00–17:00
Baga Beach season
Shack season officially opens 1 September, but in practice North Goa shacks fill in through October, and water sports operators restart when the sea is declared safe. Expect red flags and a swimming ban. Plan this as a walk-and-eat afternoon; if parasailing and jet skis are running, treat that as a bonus.
20:00
Club Tito's, Tito's Lane
Bollywood and EDM, VIP table, mocktails. Sunday of a shoulder-season week — call to confirm they're running and whether the table minimum applies.

3PANJIM
Mon 28 Sep

Latin Quarter and the river

The longest riding day and the best one for rain — almost everything here works wet. Route reordered to stop the river backtracking.

07:45
Depart Baga for Panjim
Sunrise is 06:24. An early start buys you soft light and empty lanes in Fontainhas.
08:30
Fontainhas walk
Tobacco Square, Chapel of St. Sebastian, then Confeitaria 31 De Janeiro for coffee and a Swiss roll. Park once and walk — the lanes are one-way and tight.
11:00
Fabs Bebinca, Ribandar reordered
Fatima's home-baked bebinca. Not signposted — ask near the Ribandar church. Now before Old Goa rather than after, since Ribandar sits between Panjim and Old Goa.
12:00
Basilica of Bom Jesus + Sé Cathedral
UNESCO-listed, both free. Shoulders and knees covered. The Basilica closes to visitors during Mass. Check the day's Mass times on arrival and see Sé first if you've landed in the middle of one.
14:30–17:30
Divar Island via the Old Goa jetty corrected
Free flat-bottom ferry for two-wheelers from the Old Goa terminal by the Viceroy's Arch — free for bikes, roughly 06:00 to midnight. Quiet interior roads, no traffic, good riding. Ferries can be suspended in heavy rain or rough water. Ask at the jetty what time the last crossing back runs, and don't be on the island without knowing that number.
19:30
Vinayak Family Restaurant, Assagao
Goan thali and sol kadhi. Open till 23:00. Roughly on the way home from the river.

Forts and the waterfront

Short riding day. The only real question is whether LPK is open on a Tuesday this early in the season.

09:30

Ramparts and cistern, free to enter, best light around 10:00. The lighthouse keeps its own restricted hours and the old jail section is closed to the public. You ride through Candolim to reach Aguada and come back through it afterwards. That's deliberate — morning light on the ramparts is worth the doubling back.

20:00

Mud-and-stone architecture, riverside deck, deep house. The standout venue of the trip when it's running. A Tuesday in late September is the least likely night of your whole itinerary to be open. Call, and check Instagram the same week. If it's shut, Tuesday becomes a Panjim casino-boat night instead — see the rain plan.

5SUNSET
Wed 30 Sep

Market, fort, sunset

The day that needed the most rework. The flea market anchor is probably not there yet, so the day now leads with a market that is open year-round.

09:30–12:00
Mapusa Market replaces Anjuna
Open year-round, genuinely local, and 15 minutes from Baga. Spices, cashews, produce, chorizo, bebinca. Call an Anjuna vendor or the shack owners first. If the flea market stalls are up — reopening is usually early October — go there instead and keep Mapusa in your pocket. Anjuna runs Wednesdays only, so this is your single shot at it.
13:00–15:30
Chapora Fort
Free, unstaffed ruin on a laterite headland. Views over the Chapora river mouth toward Morjim. The path up is bare laterite and it is genuinely slippery after rain. Proper shoes, not flip-flops.
17:00
Thalassa, Vagator reserve
Clifftop Greek, the sunset table of North Goa. Sunset on 30 September is 18:24, with usable light until about 18:46 — so the photo window is roughly 17:50–18:25. Thalassa has run more than one location. When you call +91 98500 33537, confirm which site you're booked at, that they're open in September, and get the reservation by message so you have it in writing.

Wet-weather riding, non-negotiable

  • No riding in active heavy rain. Pull in, wait it out, order coffee. Goan downpours are short.
  • Painted road markings, manhole covers, and diesel patches at junctions are the hazard, not the rain itself.
  • Ponchos, not umbrellas. Dry bag or bin liner for phones and passports.
  • Double the following distance in the convoy. Brake in a straight line, not mid-corner.
Ground rules

Eight people, four scooters

Four licensed riders and four bikes means no rotation and no unlicensed riding — which is the right setup. These are the rules that keep it that way.

Before you take the keys

  • Video all four scooters, all four sides, before paying.
  • Eight helmets. Goa enforces pillion helmets and traffic police in North Goa check.
  • Photograph the RC book and insurance for each bike; keep it on your phone.
  • Never hand over an original licence or passport as deposit — photocopy plus cash.
  • Check tyre tread and brakes yourself. Monsoon roads punish bald tyres.
  • Agree the fuel-return level and photograph the gauge.

Convoy discipline

  • Only the four licensed riders ride. No swapping "just for a bit".
  • Nobody rides alone at night. Four bikes leave together and arrive together.
  • Last bike sets the pace; front bike checks mirrors at every turn.
  • Share live location in one group chat for every night ride.
  • Cows on the road after dark are the classic North Goa crash. Ride to your headlight.
  • Zero alcohol for riders. The plan is already built around it — keep it that way.
